# Welcome to the OWASP Student Chapter at RV University

**Chapter Launch Date:** March 12, 2025

The OWASP Student Chapter at RV University is dedicated to fostering cybersecurity awareness, ethical hacking practices, and secure software development among students. As part of the global OWASP community, the chapter serves as a platform for learning, collaboration, and innovation in application security.

---

## Our Mission

Our mission is to promote awareness and education in web application security through:

- **Technical Workshops & Training Sessions**
- **Hands-on Demonstrations & Awareness Talks**
- **Student-led Security Projects**
- **Interactive Challenges and Competitions**
- **Community-driven Learning and Ethical Practice**

## Project Presentation  
### *Impact Scanner* – Arnav Jain

Arnav Jain presented **Impact Scanner**, an AI-assisted code vulnerability scanning tool designed to analyze entire repositories and identify security weaknesses.

**Key features demonstrated:**

- Identification of insecure coding patterns  
- Severity-based vulnerability prioritization  
- Contextual explanations for better understanding  
- Automated, audit-ready security reports  

The presentation highlighted how artificial intelligence can significantly reduce manual security effort while improving accuracy and scalability. The audience actively engaged with questions related to integration, real-world usage, and future enhancements.

---

## Technical Awareness Session  
### Discovering Exposed WebcamXP Cameras – Druthi Avadhani

Druthi Avadhani delivered a powerful awareness session on identifying exposed IP cameras using Shodan.

The talk demonstrated how publicly accessible devices running WebcamXP can be discovered through internet-wide scanning, often revealing login panels or live feeds due to misconfigurations.

**Common causes of exposure discussed:**

- Unsafe port forwarding  
- Default credentials left unchanged  
- Lack of firewall protection  
- Weak remote access configurations  

**Preventive measures emphasized:**

- Avoid exposing devices directly to the public internet  
- Change default passwords immediately  
- Enforce strong authentication mechanisms  
- Use firewalls and VPN-based remote access  

Strong emphasis was placed on **ethical responsibility**, clarifying that the objective was awareness—not exploitation.

## Interactive Competition  
### Lakera Gandalf Prompt Injection Challenge  
*(Hosted by Suviksha V B & Harnith S L)*

The final technical segment was an interactive, gamified challenge inspired by the Lakera Gandalf prompt injection exercise.

Participants explored creative prompt-engineering techniques to understand how AI safeguards can be tested and strengthened in a controlled environment.

The competition made complex AI security concepts engaging, practical, and memorable.
